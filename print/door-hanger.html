Door hanger

Two artboards: front, then back. Trim 4.25 × 11 in with 0.125 in bleed. The dashed circle and slot are the standard door-hanger die — identical on both faces so a double-sided cut lines up. Print with margins: None, scale 100%, and Background graphics ON. Text exports as vector, so there is no resolution limit. The dashed line is the trim edge — keep anything that must survive the cut inside it.
