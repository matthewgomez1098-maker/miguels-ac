What's included

What a real tune-up includes

A tune-up should not be a filter change and a sticker. We wash the condenser coil, check refrigerant charge against superheat and subcooling, test capacitor microfarads against the rating, measure amp draw on the compressor and blower, clear and treat the condensate line, tighten every electrical connection, and check the temperature split across the coil. Then we tell you what we found — including the parts that are still fine.

  • Two visits a year: cooling tune-up in spring, heating check in fall
